A practice room for everyday ethics

Practise making your case. Get feedback on how you argued.

Pick a question, take a side, and debate an AI opponent for three turns. A separate judge then scores both of you on the same five criteria.

Step 1

Choose a question

Choose a hand-reviewed dilemma or ask Claude to create a fresh, low-stakes one. There's no right answer here; you're practising the argument, not the opinion.

    Suggest your own question

    Your question is checked before the debate starts and may be declined. Counterpoint keeps practice debates away from politics, elections, group rights, health, law, money, violence and safety topics. Checks can make mistakes in either direction.

        About Counterpoint, its limits, and research notes

        How it works

        • Live mode calls Claude on the viewer's own Claude account, after the viewer allows it. The opponent and the judge are separate calls with separate instructions; the judge sees the speakers only as "Debater A" and "Debater B".
        • Demo mode uses pre-written opponent turns and an illustrative assessment. Everything it shows is labelled and nothing in it is a model response to your text.
        • The winner is computed in the page from the rubric totals, not chosen by the model.
        • Excerpts the judge quotes are checked against the transcript; any that don't appear word for word are marked.
        • AI judging is imperfect and can be inconsistent. Treat scores as practice feedback, not a grade.

        Design choices that follow from them

        • Anonymised speakers, a length-neutral instruction and a fixed rubric to reduce judge bias; the AI's own score is never self-assigned.
        • A rubric criterion for responding to the other side, because rebuttal is the skill the dialogue practises.
        • Mode is always visible, demo content is labelled, and a report control lets people flag unfair assessments.
        • 44 px controls, a docked composer with scroll padding so focused content isn't hidden, colour paired with shapes (■ you, ● AI opponent).
